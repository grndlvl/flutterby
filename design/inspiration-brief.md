# Flutterby Studio — research and inspiration brief

Research date: 2026-07-22

## Confirmed facts

- Display names used publicly: Flutterby Studio; Flutterby Photography by Amber.
- Photographer: Amber (she/her), a stay-at-home mother of two girls, artist, and photographer.
- Location shown on the Facebook page: Grovetown, Georgia.
- Current focus: photography services; booking is handled by direct message.
- Contact email shown on the promotion artwork: flutterbystudio13@gmail.com.

- Current public promotion: four discounted sessions requiring only the $25 retainer to book; availability must be confirmed.

The guide also lists a non-refundable retainer and optional USB/photo-album extras. Confirm all terms before launch.

## Brand assets and palette

The supplied brand art uses delicate watercolor flowers, a butterfly line drawing, a warm oatmeal ground, and lavender/olive accents. Supplied palette: `#a094b8`, `#9d95ae`, `#dfd9e9`, `#ffffff`, `#dccbb7`, `#b4a28c`, `#c5c38c`, `#4a4d28`, and `#7e7c5a`. Pale colors are surfaces only; dark olive/ink is used for readable text.

Strong current patterns include an emotionally specific promise above the fold, work before a long biography, scannable session categories, one repeated booking action, reassurance for camera-shy clients, and a mobile-friendly editorial gallery. Avoid generic beige “luxury photographer” styling, inaccessible script body copy, autoplay galleries, long self-focused introductions, low-contrast text over photographs, and invented testimonials.

## Original direction

“Artful wonder, honest connection.” The site should feel bright, tactile, and personal rather than formal or luxurious. Photographs lead; botanical and butterfly cues remain restrained. Amber's unusual and credible promise—helping people see themselves with tenderness—is the conversion story. Use an editorial serif with a clear sans-serif body, asymmetric image rhythm, warm paper, deep olive ink, and lavender details.
