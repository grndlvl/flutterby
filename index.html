Meet the photographer

Hi, I’m Amber.

I’m a stay-at-home mom to two girls, an artist, and the person behind the camera at Flutterby Studio, based in Grovetown and serving families throughout Augusta, North Augusta, and the wider CSRA. As a little girl, I dreamed of photographing for National Geographic and traveling the world. Over the last seven years, I’ve fallen just as deeply in love with photographing people—their personalities, emotions, and connections.

I know what it’s like to struggle with photos of yourself. My seven-year-old took a portrait of me that made me cry because I loved it so much. That feeling—surprise, recognition, and a little more love for yourself—is what I hope every client takes home.
